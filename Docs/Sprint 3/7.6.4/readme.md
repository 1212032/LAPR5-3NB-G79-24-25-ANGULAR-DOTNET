The legal bases for processing your data include:

- Rectification: Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Erasure: Request deletion of your data where legally permissible.
- Restriction: Request a limitation on how we process your data.
- Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
- Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.
- Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on your consent.
