LAPR5 S.A. Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01-01-2025

LAPR5 S.A is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of its patients, employees, and other stakeholders. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Portuguese laws.

What is Personal Data and What Categories of Data Do We Process?

Personal Data refers to any information that identifies or can identify an individual person.

At LAPR5 S.A., we may process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identification Data: Name, address, date of birth, identification documents, and contact details.
  • Health Data: Medical history, diagnosis, treatment information, and laboratory results.
  • Financial Data: Payment information, insurance details, and billing records.
  • Administrative Data: Appointment schedules, consents, and contractual details.
  • Sensitive Data: Genetic information or other data requiring special protection.

In What Situations Do We Process Your Personal Data?

We may process your personal data in the following situations:

  • When you visit us for medical treatment, consultation or surgery.
  • When you use the appointment booking.
  • When you communicate with us via mobile, email, or in person.
  • When fulfilling legal or regulatory obligations.
  • When processing employment-related data for staff and contractors.

For What Purposes Do We Process Your Personal Data?

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Medical Care: To provide accurate diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up care.
  • Administrative Activities: Managing appointments, billing, and insurance claims.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Adhering to Portuguese healthcare laws and GDPR requirements.
  • Research and Education: Conducting anonymized research to improve healthcare services.
  • Employment Management: Managing payroll, contracts, and employee benefits.
  • Emergency Situations: Providing necessary care in urgent scenarios.

On What Basis Do We Process Your Personal Data?

The legal bases for processing your data include:

  • Consent: When you provide explicit consent for specific purposes (e.g., research participation).
  • Contractual Obligations: When processing is necessary to fulfill a contract (e.g., employment contracts).
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with national healthcare regulations and legal obligations.
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve operations and patient care, provided such interests do not override your fundamental rights.
  • Vital Interests: In emergencies where processing data is necessary to protect life.

How Long Do We Keep Your Personal Data?

Your personal data will be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes stated above, in compliance with legal and regulatory requirements:

  • Medical Records: Kept for a minimum of 20 years, as mandated by Portuguese law.
  • Financial Data: Retained for 10 years for tax compliance.
  • Research Data: Retained until anonymization or explicit consent withdrawal.
  • Employment Records: Retained for the duration of employment plus an additional statutory period.

Who Do We Pass On Your Personal Data To?

We may share your data with:

  • Healthcare Providers: Specialists, laboratories, and external medical consultants involved in your care.
  • Regulatory Authorities: To comply with mandatory reporting obligations.
  • Insurance Companies: For processing claims with your explicit consent.
  • IT Service Providers: For secure data storage and management systems.
  • Emergency Services: If necessary to protect your health or safety.

We ensure that all third parties comply with GDPR standards and our data protection policies.

What Are Your Personal Data Protection Rights and How Can You Exercise Them?

Under GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data where legally permissible.
  • Restriction: Request a limitation on how we process your data.
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on your consent.
